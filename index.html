Milwaukee doula for birth, high‑risk pregnancy & NICU families

I'm Amanda, and I'm glad you're here!

I'm a certified doula with a doctorate in public health and over a decade of experience in maternal and child health. I believe birth looks different for everyone, and there's no single "right" way to do it. Birth stays with us for the rest of our lives, and I want yours to be a positive start for you and your baby.

Whether you're hoping for an unmedicated birth, planning an epidural, preparing for an induction or a scheduled cesarean, or aren't sure yet what you want, I offer the same respectful support. I have particular expertise in supporting families through high-risk pregnancies and NICU admissions.

What I Offer

Birth & Pregnancy Support

Support through pregnancy, birth, and the early weeks: preparing for labor, writing your birth preferences, support during labor and birth, and getting ready for life with a newborn.

Research links doula support to better birth experiences and more confidence as a new parent.

See packages

High-Risk & Complicated Pregnancy

If your pregnancy is high-risk or complicated, whether that means preeclampsia, gestational diabetes, twins, concerns about preterm birth, or a diagnosis for your baby, I can support you in preparing for appointments, making decisions, and planning for your birth and recovery.

NICU Support

The NICU team cares for your baby. I'm here for you, from before birth through the stay and the transition home.

I'm an Ohio native and have lived in Milwaukee with my family since 2021. You'll usually find me taking a walk through Humboldt Park, reading a sci-fi romance, or baking a gluten-free treat.

Why "Lark"? The boring answer is that it's the word I came up with when I mashed up my last name with my husband's. It resonated with me because larks are songbirds, and my daughter, Melody, is why I do this work. I hope it resonates with you because larks symbolize new beginnings and self-discovery, a journey I hope we can embark on together.
