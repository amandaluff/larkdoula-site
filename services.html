Services & Pricing

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a doula do, and what don't you do?

A doula provides emotional, informational, and practical support. I help you understand your options, prepare questions for your care team, and feel supported in your decisions. I do not provide medical care, give medical advice, or make decisions for you. I work alongside your doctors, midwives, and nurses, not in place of them.

How do you support high-risk or complicated pregnancies?

Complicated pregnancies and planned medical interventions mean you have to make many decisions, and it can be hard to navigate on your own. Your doctor or midwife visits may be too short to answer all your questions, and their main focus has to be on your medical care and physical safety. That does not always leave room for the support you might need. My role is to help you understand the information your clinical team provides, talk through your options, and help you plan what you want your pregnancy, birth, and postpartum period to look like.

More about high-risk pregnancy support

Is a doula still helpful if I'm having an induction, an epidural, or a planned cesarean?

Yes. Support isn't only for unmedicated births. I can help you prepare for what to expect, stay with you through a long induction, support you and your partner before and after a cesarean, and help you recover and adjust afterward.

More about support for planned interventions

What does a NICU doula do?

Having a baby in the NICU is a lot to process. The hospital team is there to give your baby the best possible care, but it is easy for you to feel a bit lost or unsure of your place. My role is to focus on your family. I give you a safe space to talk through how you are feeling, help you find ways to connect with your baby, and provide support so you do not have to navigate the NICU alone.

More about NICU doula support

Do you offer payment plans?

Payment plans are available for some services, but the first payment is due before your first visit. For prenatal and birth doula services, payment is due in full by 37 weeks.

What does text and email support include?

Text and email support allow you to reach out between visits with questions, updates, concerns, or decisions you're working through. This support is intended for non-urgent issues and does not replace medical care. I typically respond during daytime and evening hours.

Do you offer reduced fee services?

I am happy to work with families experiencing financial need, as well as Black, Indigenous, and LGBTQ+ families, to discuss a support plan that fits your needs and your budget.

Where do you provide services?

I am happy to meet you for visits in your home, at your hospital, or in a public place. I also offer all prenatal and postpartum visits virtually. I am based in Bay View and serve families across Milwaukee County, including Wauwatosa, Whitefish Bay, Glendale, Greenfield, West Allis, Franklin, Oak Creek, South Milwaukee, and Cudahy. I can attend births at any local hospital or birth center, including Aurora Sinai Medical Center, Aurora West Allis Medical Center, Ascension Columbia St. Mary's Hospital Milwaukee, Ascension St. Joseph, and the Froedtert Hospital Birth Center.
