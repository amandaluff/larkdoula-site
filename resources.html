Community Resource Guide

Welcome to the Lark Doula community resource guide. Here, Milwaukee-area families can find local support for infant feeding, mental health, parenting, and loss.

Breastfeeding

Wisco Lactation

Comprehensive lactation support with multiple locations in the Milwaukee and Madison, Wisconsin areas. Staffed by IBCLCs, and additionally offering services such as bodywork and maternal nutrition consultation. Individual services available in person or virtually, as well as classes and groups. Insurance accepted.

Address: 530 N 108th Pl. Suite 203, Wauwatosa, WI 53226 (other locations in Kenosha, Sun Prairie, Verona)

African American Breastfeeding Network

AABN was established in 2008 to improve maternal child health and champion breastfeeding equity by advocating system/policy changes, partnering with community allies and implementing community-based, family-centered, culturally tailored health education and support services. Offers prenatal breastfeeding education and postpartum breastfeeding support from Community Lactation Specialists.

Address: 619 W Walnut St, Milwaukee, WI 53212

Maternal Mental Health

Mom’s Mental Health Initiative

Free resource brokering and peer support services available to pregnant and postpartum people in Southeast Wisconsin and Dane County. Connects people to in-person and virtual mental health services in the region. Also offers a private online support group for pregnant and postpartum people.

Parental Support

The Parenting Network Milwaukee

Offers parenting classes, support groups, and resources for postpartum support to Milwaukee families, with a focus on underserved communities.

Address: Mailing Address: 7516 West Burleigh Street, Milwaukee, WI 53210 / Parent Helpline: (414) 671-0566

Grief & Loss

African American Breastfeeding Network (Healing Waters Program)

The AABN Healing Waters program supports families who have experienced the loss of a pregnancy at any stage, or the death of a baby up to 12 months of age. A trained Perinatal Loss Counselor provides one-on-one support to families who have experienced loss.

Address: 619 W Walnut St, Milwaukee, WI 53212

Wisconsin Center for Stillbirth and Infant Death

Provides free support to families after a stillbirth or other infant loss. Available services include individualized guidance from Family Support Coordinators, online support groups, 24/7 grief support lines, funeral and financial assistance connections, and resources for counseling, therapy, and bereavement photography.

Contact: 920-561-1600 / wicsid@starlegacyfoundation.org

Parents of Children with Special Needs

Parent to Parent Wisconsin

Helps parents find a one-to-one connection with another parent who knows firsthand about the feelings and realities that come with having a child with special needs.

Contact: (414) 266-5608 / P2PWI@childrenswi.org
