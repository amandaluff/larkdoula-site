Community Resource Guide

Local and national support for Milwaukee-area families: mental health, breastfeeding, parenting, NICU families, a new diagnosis, grief and loss, and basic needs.

In a crisis, call or text 988, or call the Milwaukee County Crisis Line at (414) 257-7222 (24 hours, any age). In an emergency, call 911.

Maternal Mental Health

Mom’s Mental Health Initiative

Free resource brokering and peer support services available to pregnant and postpartum people in Southeast Wisconsin and Dane County. Connects people to in-person and virtual mental health services in the region. Also offers a private online support group for pregnant and postpartum people.

Postpartum Support International (PSI)

National support for depression, anxiety, and other mood changes during pregnancy and after birth, for parents and partners. The HelpLine connects you with support and resources, and a trained volunteer returns your call or text. Also offers more than 50 online support groups led by trained facilitators and a directory of perinatal mental health providers. Available in English and Spanish. The HelpLine is not a crisis line; in an emergency, call 911 or call or text 988.

Phone: 1-800-944-4773; text 800-944-4773 (English) or 971-203-7773 (Español)

National Maternal Mental Health Hotline

Free, confidential support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, in English and Spanish, for anyone who is pregnant or has had a baby. Trained counselors listen, connect you with local support groups, and refer you to other care if you need it. Partners and family members can use it too.

Phone: 1-833-TLC-MAMA (1-833-852-6262), call or text

Breastfeeding

Wisco Lactation

Lactation support from IBCLCs, with locations in the Milwaukee and Madison areas. Offers one-on-one visits in person or virtually, classes, and groups, plus bodywork and maternal nutrition consultations. Insurance accepted. Also offers free infant feeding support groups in Milwaukee.

Address: 530 N 108th Pl. Suite 203, Wauwatosa, WI 53226 (other locations in Kenosha, Sun Prairie, Verona)
Phone: (414) 249-4420 (call or text)

African American Breastfeeding Network

Offers prenatal breastfeeding education and postpartum breastfeeding support from Community Lactation Specialists. Hosts a Community Breastfeeding Gathering, in person or virtual, open to parents, partners, family, and friends, and one-on-one support with a lactation consultant. Registration is required for both.

Address: 619 W Walnut St, Milwaukee, WI 53212
Phone: (414) 207-6728

Parent Support

The Parenting Network Milwaukee

Offers parenting classes, support groups, and resources for postpartum support to Milwaukee families, with a focus on underserved communities.

Phone: (414) 671-0566 (Parent Helpline)

NICU Families

March of Dimes NICU Family Support at Children's Wisconsin

For families with a baby in the Children's Wisconsin NICU in Milwaukee. A family support specialist offers education sessions, events, and support for NICU parents, siblings, and grandparents. Ask your baby's nurse to connect you.

Hand to Hold

A national nonprofit offering free emotional support for families before, during, and after a NICU stay. Offers online support groups, a peer mentor program that matches you with a parent who has been through the NICU, counseling, podcasts, and support after a loss. Available in English and Spanish.

Ronald McDonald House Charities Eastern Wisconsin

A place to stay near Children's Wisconsin and the Milwaukee Regional Medical Center for families whose child is in the hospital, including during a long NICU stay. Ask your baby's social worker about a referral.

Address: 8948 Watertown Plank Road, Milwaukee, WI 53226
Phone: (414) 475-5333

Navigating a Pediatric Diagnosis

Parent to Parent of Wisconsin

For parents of a child with a disability, a health condition, or other special health care needs. Matches you one-to-one with a trained volunteer parent who has been through something similar, based on things like your child's diagnosis and age, your language, and where you live. Run through Children's Wisconsin.

Phone: (833) 361-6300 (toll free) or (414) 266-5608 (en español)
Email: P2PWI@childrenswi.org

Hope for HIE

A national nonprofit for families of babies and children with hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy (HIE), a brain injury caused by a lack of oxygen or blood flow to the brain, usually around birth. Offers virtual support groups (including pregnancy after HIE and loss), online family groups, including one for families new to HIE, and care boxes for families in the NICU.

Phone: (248) 238-8112

Milwaukee County Birth to 3

Early intervention for babies and toddlers up to age 3 who have a developmental delay or a diagnosis likely to cause one. After a referral, a service coordinator and evaluation team work with your family, and visits can happen wherever you're comfortable. A free online screening is also available.

Phone: (414) 289-6799 for referrals, Monday to Friday (Spanish available)

Grief & Loss

African American Breastfeeding Network (Healing Waters Program)

The AABN Healing Waters program supports families who have experienced the loss of a pregnancy at any stage, or the death of a baby up to 12 months of age. A trained Perinatal Loss Counselor provides one-on-one support to families who have experienced loss.

Address: 619 W Walnut St, Milwaukee, WI 53212
Phone: (414) 207-6728

Wisconsin Center for Stillbirth and Infant Death

Provides free support to families after a stillbirth or other infant loss. Available services include individualized guidance from Family Support Coordinators, online support groups, 24/7 grief support lines, funeral and financial assistance connections, and resources for counseling, therapy, and bereavement photography.

Phone: (920) 561-1600
Email: wicsid@starlegacyfoundation.org

Basic Needs & Safety

211 Wisconsin

Free, confidential help finding local services, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Trained specialists can connect you with food pantries, housing and utility help, mental health support, domestic violence services, and other local programs. You can also search for services by ZIP code on their website.

Phone: 211

WIC (City of Milwaukee Health Department)

Healthy food, nutrition education, and breastfeeding support for pregnant and postpartum people, babies, and young children, for families who qualify by income. Breastfeeding help comes from trained breastfeeding experts and peer counselors, and WIC can help you get a breast pump. Fathers and other caregivers can apply for their children.

Address: Northwest Health Center, 7630 W Mill Rd; Southside Health Center, 1639 S 23rd St; Keenan Health Center, 3200 N 36th St, Milwaukee
Phone: (414) 286-8820

Bay View Community Center Food Pantry

A free emergency food pantry where you choose your own groceries, including refrigerated and frozen foods. Open to households in the 53207, 53215, 53221, and 53235 ZIP codes, once a month. Bring ID for everyone in your household and proof of address. Open Mondays and Wednesdays 4 to 6 pm, and Saturdays 11 am to 1:30 pm.

Distribution site for the Milwaukee Diaper Mission. Visit their website for sites serving other ZIP codes.

Address: 1320 E Oklahoma Ave, Milwaukee, WI 53207
Phone: (414) 482-1000

Milwaukee LGBT Community Center

Milwaukee's LGBTQ+ community center. Offers a food pantry, a gender-affirming clothing boutique, personal care items, and mental health support and activities. Basic needs hours are Tuesdays and Thursdays, 3 to 6 pm.

Address: 161 S 1st St, Suite 100, Milwaukee, WI 53204
Phone: (414) 271-2656

Children's Wisconsin Car Seat Safety

Certified car seat technicians check or install your car seat by appointment. Low-cost car seats are available on a limited basis for families who need one. For a special needs or medical car seat, email for guidance.

Phone: (414) 607-5280
Email: carseat@childrenswi.org (special needs car seats)

Sojourner Family Peace Center

Milwaukee's domestic violence center. Offers a 24-hour hotline, emergency shelter, and help with legal and other services for people experiencing domestic violence and their children.

Phone: (414) 933-2722 (24-hour hotline); text (414) 877-8100

A Better Balance

A national nonprofit with a free, confidential legal helpline about your rights at work and at school during pregnancy and after birth, including accommodations, pumping at work, family and medical leave, and discrimination for being pregnant or a parent. Available in English and Spanish, and LGBTQ-inclusive. You can call or fill out the form on their website.

Phone: 1-833-NEED-ABB (1-833-633-3222)
