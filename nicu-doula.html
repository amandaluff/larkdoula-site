What a NICU doula does

Having a baby in the NICU is a lot to process. The hospital team is there to give your baby the best possible care, but it is easy for you to feel a bit lost or unsure of your place.

My role is to focus on your family. I give you a safe space to talk through how you are feeling, help you find ways to connect with your baby, and provide support so you do not have to navigate the NICU alone.

How I can help

  • Understanding what your baby's care team is telling you, and preparing questions for rounds.
  • Connecting with your baby: finding ways to bond and take part in their care.
  • Recovering: looking after your own recovery while your baby is in the hospital.
  • Going home: preparing for discharge and the first weeks at home.

Where I work

I support families with babies in any Milwaukee-area NICU, including Children's Wisconsin, Aurora Sinai, Aurora West Allis, Ascension Columbia St. Mary's, and Ascension St. Joseph. Visits can happen at the hospital (as policy allows), at home, or virtually.
