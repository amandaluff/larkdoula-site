Privacy

Your privacy and trust mean everything to me. This page explains what information this website collects and how I use it.

Information you choose to share

When you complete the contact form, call, text, or email me, I receive the details you choose to give, such as your name, how to reach you, and what kind of support you are looking for.

Any information you share is kept confidential and will only be used to respond to you and to help me provide the best support for your family. The contact form is handled by a form service that emails your responses to me.

Information collected automatically

This website uses Google Analytics to help me understand how people find and use the site. It records things like which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors are located (city level), what kind of device they use, and how they arrived. It also records when someone clicks my phone number, my email address, or a Reach Out button, and when the contact form is sent.

Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. It does not tell me your name or email address, and it cannot see what you type into the contact form.

This website also uses the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta, the company behind Facebook and Instagram. It tells Meta which pages are visited, so I can see whether my Facebook and Instagram posts and ads bring people to this site. Meta may connect a visit with your Facebook or Instagram account and use it under Meta's privacy policy.

If you would rather not be counted, you can install Google's free Analytics opt-out browser add-on, turn off ads based on your activity in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings, or block cookies in your browser. The site works the same either way.

How your information is shared

I do not sell your information. Anything you share with me is treated as confidential.

There are limited situations where I may share information:

  • With a backup doula. If you become a client, I may share relevant details with a backup doula so you are supported if I am unavailable. This only happens after a contract is signed, and you would be informed.
  • When I am legally or ethically required to. For example, if there is a serious concern about someone's safety.

The services that run this website process information only as described on this page.

Your choices

You can ask me what information I have about you, or ask me to delete it, at any time. Email amanda@larkdoula.com or call/text (414) 285-3445.

Changes to this page

If how I handle information changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
