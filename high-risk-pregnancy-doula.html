Doula Support for High‑Risk & Complicated Pregnancies

If your pregnancy is high-risk or complicated, I can help you understand what's going on and think through your questions and decisions.

Reach Out

Why extra support helps

Complicated pregnancies and planned medical interventions mean you have to make many decisions, and it can be hard to navigate on your own.

Your doctor or midwife visits may be too short to answer all your questions, and their main focus has to be on your medical care and physical safety. That does not always leave room for the support you might need.

I support families facing preeclampsia, gestational diabetes, twins, concerns about preterm birth, or a diagnosis they're still making sense of.

What to expect from me

What I do

My role is to help you understand the information your clinical team provides, talk through your options, and help you plan what you want your pregnancy, birth, and postpartum period to look like. I'm also someone to talk to about how things are going.

What I don't do

I do not provide medical care, give medical advice, or make decisions for you. I work alongside your doctors, midwives, and nurses, not in place of them.

Inductions, epidurals, and planned cesareans

Support isn't only for unmedicated births.

I can help you prepare for what to expect, stay with you through a long induction, support you and your partner before and after a cesarean, and help you recover and adjust afterward.

I can attend births at any local hospital or birth center, including Aurora Sinai Medical Center, Aurora West Allis Medical Center, Ascension Columbia St. Mary's Hospital Milwaukee, Ascension St. Joseph, and the Froedtert Hospital Birth Center.
