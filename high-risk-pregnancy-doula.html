High‑Risk Pregnancy Doula Support

A pregnancy can become high-risk or complicated for many reasons: high blood pressure or preeclampsia, diabetes, twins, concerns about preterm birth, or a diagnosis for your baby. Sometimes it's a past loss or a hard previous birth that makes this pregnancy feel different. Some of these pregnancies also mean planning an induction or a cesarean. Whatever brought you here, this support is for you.

How I can help

  • Understanding your care: making sense of what your doctor or midwife tells you, and preparing questions for your next appointment.
  • Planning your birth: talking through your options and writing your birth preferences, including for an induction or a planned cesarean.
  • At the birth: however your birth happens, I can help with comfort and decisions, and support your partner.
  • After the birth: help as you recover and adjust. If your baby needs extra care, see NICU doula support.

I don't provide medical care, give medical advice, or make decisions for you.

Where I work

I can attend births at any local hospital or birth center, including Aurora Sinai Medical Center, Aurora West Allis Medical Center, Ascension Columbia St. Mary's Hospital Milwaukee, Ascension St. Joseph, and the Froedtert Hospital Birth Center. Visits can happen at the hospital, at home, or virtually.
