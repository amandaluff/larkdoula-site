Pregnancy Loss, Infant Loss, and Abortion Support

I offer support for pregnancy and infant loss and for abortion at no cost, in person in Milwaukee County or virtually.

As a doula, I can provide emotional and practical support as you navigate this process, but I am not a therapist or counselor. I don't provide medical care, give medical advice, or make decisions for you.

Pregnancy and infant loss

I offer support at no cost for miscarriage, stillbirth, loss for medical reasons (TFMR), and infant loss. Support can include:

  • Planning ahead when a loss is expected
  • Information about what to expect
  • In-person support
  • Lactation support after a loss
  • Support for partners and family

Abortion support

I offer private support at no cost before, during, and after an abortion. I'll respect whatever you decide. You're welcome to share as little or as much as you want. I won't record your name or any personal details, and if you'd prefer, you can reach me on Signal.

Support can include:

  • Talking through what to expect
  • Being with you at home during a medication abortion
  • Support at your appointments, when the clinic allows it
  • Checking in with you afterward
