Research Corner

Decoding the science of birth and maternal health.

Welcome to Doula, PhD, an evidence-based resource library by Lark Doula.

It can be difficult to separate medical evidence from personal opinions about childbirth. Here, I break down peer-reviewed research, clinical trials, and public health data into plain language, whether you are exploring options for an unmedicated birth, trying to understand the evidence behind specific medical interventions, or looking into the impact of continuous labor support.

Written by a certified doula with a doctorate in public health, Doula, PhD provides the data you need to advocate for yourself and make informed, confident choices about your care.
